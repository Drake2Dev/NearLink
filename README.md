# NearLink
The Dynamic NFC Digital Wallet for Android

## Full Description:

Turn your Android phone into a powerful, tap-to-share digital business card and link broadcaster.

NearLink transforms your device into a customizable NFC tag using advanced Host Card Emulation (HCE) technology. Whether you are networking at a conference, sharing your creative portfolio, or passing along a business website, NearLink lets you beam any URL directly to another NFC-enabled smartphone with a simple tap. Say goodbye to carrying stacks of paper business cards and hello to seamless, contactless sharing.

**Core Features:**

৹ Digital Wallet Interface: Organize all your important URLs in a clean, minimalist, and easy-to-read list of customizable cards.  

৹ One-Tap Broadcasting: Tap any card to instantly activate your NFC broadcast. The active card turns a vibrant green, giving you clear visual confirmation of exactly which link is currently being shared. Tap it again to stop broadcasting instantly.

৹ Quick Management: Easily tweak or remove links on a card by press and holding the card and then choosing to edit or delete.

৹ Clipboard Export & Import: Easily back up your entire link library or transfer your setup to a new device.

৹ Dark Mode Optimization: Select light and dark themes for a comfortable viewing experience in any environment.

৹ 100% Local & Secure: Your data belongs to you. All links and configurations are saved locally to your device's persistent memory. NearLink requires no account creation, no subscriptions, and no external cloud servers.

৹ Ad-Free: This app is freely given away with no ads or telemetry data being collected. 

**Perfect For:**

৹ Professionals & Entrepreneurs: Share your company website, LinkedIn profile, or digital contact card instantly. 

৹ Creatives & Freelancers: Beam your digital portfolio or link-in-bio directly to potential clients.

৹ Events & Retail: Quickly direct users to event schedules, promotional pages, or digital menus.
